1 The window

A normal Mac window (v73): red hides it (the Dock icon or the menu bar icon brings it back), yellow minimises it to the Dock, drag the title bar to move it, an edge to make it wider. Settings: ⌘,.

Q Open · Quit

Open = the Mac goes out through Frankfurt (bonded). ⌘Q = back to the Mac's normal connection, nothing of the app left (new connections never fail; the same if the app crashes). OFF = stop everything, also at the next login.

2 WI-FI · CABLE · BOTH

Which lines the Mac uses: only Wi-Fi, only cables and phones, or BOTH joined together (aggregation). A click on the word lists the networks or cables to tick.

3 Cloud

Claude Code's connection to its server: green when it answers, red when calls drop.

4 Tailscale

The guard that restarts Tailscale when it is stuck, so the Mac stays reachable from anywhere.

5 ON · OFF

OFF stops everything the app put between the Mac and the internet, then quits. ON is the app running.

6 ⚙ Settings

Units, probes, the Claude watch, and the other switches.

7 ⟳ Rescan

Find every cable and phone again, e.g. just after plugging in a phone.

8 The connection

The line the internet goes through now. A click switches one connection ⇄ aggregate.

9 ↓ ↑ Live speed

Green ↓ download, red ↑ upload, measured every second.

10 Ping

The round trip to the internet; a click pings again.

11 Data

The SIM data left (read from Telemach by SMS).

12 ⏲ Speed test

Opens speedtest.net in the app's own window, no ads. Press GO. The icon again (or Esc) closes it.

13 Health

Every line at once: ping, jitter, loss, HTTPS times. Almost no data.

H Help

This page. H again or Esc closes it.

T TOP

Green: the stripe stays above every window.

A ALL · IN USE

ALL: every connection the Mac can see. IN USE: only the lines carrying traffic now.

F Filters (in ALL)

🔌 LAN: only wired lines (cables and phones over USB). 📶 Wi-Fi: only Wi-Fi networks. Green = on; press again for everything.

J Click a name to join

White = not in use. Click → green = it joins the aggregated connection. Click again → it leaves.

L Meters and state

The signal (cable speed, Wi-Fi or 4G/5G strength) and one word: IN USE, READY, NO DATA, AIRPLANE, VIA WI-FI, SAME UPLINK, NO NET.

S Status line

All lines together and the downloads running; ✕ hides a line.

14 Grip

Drag the right edge: first the gaps close, then the letters shrink. Wider: gaps first, then bigger letters.

Colours

in use, working ready, not in use busy or a warning (NO DATA) upload / failure off, or cannot be used

Faster internet in four steps

Plug in the phones by USB (data on, not on Wi-Fi), press ⟳.
Open ALL, press 🔌 to see the wired lines.
Click each name until it is green.
Press ⏲ and GO to check the speed.

Good to know

One download or one video call uses one line at a time; many downloads, a browser with many tabs, or the app's own downloader use all the lines together. A phone joins only with its own mobile data (not in airplane mode, not through Wi-Fi). Big downloads switch the phones to 5G by themselves and back to 4G afterwards.
