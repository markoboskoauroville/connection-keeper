1 The window

A normal Mac window (v73): red hides it (the Dock icon or the menu bar icon brings it back), yellow minimises it to the Dock, drag the title bar to move it, an edge to make it wider. Settings: ⌘,.

Q Open · Quit

Open = the Mac goes out through Frankfurt (bonded). ⌘Q = back to the Mac's normal connection, nothing of the app left (new connections never fail; the same if the app crashes). OFF = stop everything, also at the next login.

2 One tick per connection

Every connection the app knows is listed (cables, phones on USB, Wi-Fi networks in range, lines seen this week). Ticked = part of the aggregate connection. No tick = the Mac straight on its own connection. One tick = everything through that line. A ticked Wi-Fi network is joined.

W FRANKFURT · CROATIA

Two buttons, the chosen one green. FRANKFURT: all traffic through the relay in Frankfurt; sites see Frankfurt, not your home address (one line is enough). CROATIA: straight over your own lines; sites see your home address. The switch is seamless: open connections finish the old way, new ones take the new way at once. Also in View (⇧⌘F, ⇧⌘C) and on a right click of the menu bar icon. Remembered.

B ⇄ BOND

⇄ FRA 2 · 48↓: every connection through the relay in Frankfurt, bonded over 2 lines, 48 Mbit/s coming in. ⇄ FRA 1: one line: still through Frankfurt (the home address hidden, like a VPN), no speed gain. ⇄ ASIDE (amber): switched off, draining, or one line with "Bond only when it adds speed" (⚙): new connections go direct. ⇄ DOWN: no leg to the relay. STRICT: with no leg, nothing goes over the home line. A click runs the full check (Frankfurt, legs, DNS leaks, IPv6). Domains in the Direct list (⚙) skip Frankfurt and show the home address.

3 Cloud

Claude Code's connection to its server: green when it answers, red when calls drop.

4 Tailscale

The guard that restarts Tailscale when it is stuck, so the Mac stays reachable from anywhere.

5 ON · OFF

OFF stops everything the app put between the Mac and the internet, then quits. ON is the app running.

6 ⚙ Settings

Units, probes, the Claude watch, and the other switches.

7 ⟳ Rescan

Find every cable and phone again, e.g. just after plugging in a phone.

8 The connection

The line the internet goes through now. A click switches one connection ⇄ aggregate.

9 ↓ ↑ Live speed

Green ↓ download, red ↑ upload, measured every second.

10 Ping

The round trip to the internet; a click pings again.

11 Data

The SIM data left (read from Telemach by SMS).

12 ⏲ Speed test

Opens speedtest.net in the app's own window, no ads. Press GO. The icon again (or Esc) closes it.

13 Health

Every line at once: ping, jitter, loss, HTTPS times. Almost no data.

H Help

This page. H again or Esc closes it.

T TOP

Green: the stripe stays above every window.

A ALL TICKED

The ticked lines together. Its TEST measures the combination, to compare with each line alone.

M The menu-bar item

A click opens the drop-down controller: Open Connection Keeper on top, then FRANKFURT / CROATIA, the lines here with their tick, ↓ mark, speed and state, ALL TICKED with its TEST, aggregation ON / OFF, Check now, the log, Quit. A click outside or Esc closes it. Right click: FRANKFURT / CROATIA only. Hover: the card with what it is doing.

D ↓ Downloads

The ↓ beside a tick makes that line the default download line (one line at most; it is ticked too). In CROATIA a transfer bigger than 50 MB (⚙) makes its site's next connections go to that line; ck-net's downloads use only that line. The row says DOWNLOADS, or DL: FALLBACK when the line does not answer (then the downloads are balanced as before), or DL: CROATIA ONLY in FRANKFURT. ↓ again: no download line.

X TEST

speedtest.net over this line only, in the background (about 40 s); the result and its time stay on the row. One test at a time.

J The same road cancels

Two rows that are the same road out cannot both be ticked: one Wi-Fi at a time, a phone and its own hotspot (one SIM), two lines behind one router. Ticking one unticks the other, which says why for a few seconds (⇄ SAME PHONE, ⇄ SAME ROUTER, ⇄ ONE WI-FI).

L Meters and state

The signal (cable speed, Wi-Fi or 4G/5G strength) and one word: IN USE, READY, NO DATA, AIRPLANE, VIA WI-FI, SAME UPLINK, NO NET.

S Status line

All lines together and the downloads running; ✕ hides a line.

14 Grip

Drag the right edge: first the gaps close, then the letters shrink. Wider: gaps first, then bigger letters.

Colours

in use, working ready, not in use busy or a warning (NO DATA) upload / failure off, or cannot be used

Faster internet in four steps

Plug in the phones by USB (data on, not on Wi-Fi), press ⟳.
Tick the lines you want joined (one of each road).
TEST each line, then TEST on ALL TICKED.
Press ⏲ and GO to check the speed.

Good to know

One download or one video call uses one line at a time; many downloads, a browser with many tabs, or the app's own downloader use all the lines together. A phone joins only with its own mobile data (not in airplane mode, not through Wi-Fi). Big downloads switch the phones to 5G by themselves and back to 4G afterwards.
